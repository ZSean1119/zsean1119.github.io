Project 001 / Robotics & mechanical design

Automated
Checkers Robot.

A gantry-style robotic prototype built around a checkers board, combining belt-driven translation, aluminum extrusion framing, 3D-printed components, and a vertically oriented end-effector.

01 / Overview

Bringing a tabletop game into motion.

This prototype uses a two-axis motion system to position a toolhead above a checkerboard. The assembly brings together structural aluminum members, timing belts, printed mounts, and a vertically oriented toolhead, creating a physical platform for experimenting with checkers-piece manipulation.

02 / Mechanical system

How the mechanism is built.

  • 01 — X–Y gantry motionOrthogonal belt-driven axes move the toolhead across the board's working area.
  • 02 — Custom printed hardware3D-printed mounts, supports, and carriage parts connect the moving elements to the frame.
  • 03 — Toolhead assemblyA vertical end-effector assembly is mounted to the moving carriage to interact with checkers pieces.
  • 04 — Integrated test platformThe board sits inside the working envelope of the surrounding frame for prototyping and testing.
